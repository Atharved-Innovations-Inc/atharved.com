The Problem
Self-checkout made things worse
Self-checkout was supposed to fix retail checkout. Instead, theft rose 67%, queues stayed, and shoppers hate it. The core problem remains: someone still has to scan every item.

The Solution
A cart that does the checkout for you
The Avadh Dash Cart handles the entire checkout process inside the cart itself — while the shopper shops. Every item is scanned, weighed, verified, and sorted automatically.

Drop In

Shopper places item into the cart's intake area. A conveyor moves it to the scanning station.

Scan & Weigh

Camera + barcode scanner identify the item. Load cell verifies weight against the catalog. Mismatches are flagged instantly.

Pick & Sort

A 3-axis robotic gantry picks the item and places it into the correct bin — fragile, heavy, or general — with Fin-Ray soft-grip fingers.

Pay & Go

Running total displayed on the tablet. Checkout is digital — tap to pay and walk out. No queue, no cashier, no self-scan station.
