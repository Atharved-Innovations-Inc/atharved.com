How It Works

Four steps. Zero scanning.

The cart handles the entire checkout process autonomously while the shopper walks the aisles.

01

Drop In

Shopper places item into the intake area. A conveyor draws it to the scanning station automatically.

02

Scan + Weigh

Camera and barcode scanner identify the item. Load cell verifies weight against the product catalog. Mismatches flagged instantly.

03

Pick + Sort

A 3-axis robotic gantry with soft-grip Fin-Ray fingers picks and places the item into the correct bin — fragile, heavy, or general.

04

Pay + Go

Running total on the built-in display. Tap to pay digitally and walk out. No queue, no cashier, no self-scan station.
